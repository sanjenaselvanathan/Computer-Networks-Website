Online Learning

Computer networks connect students, teachers, computers and online learning platforms. They allow students to access educational materials, attend virtual classes, submit assignments and communicate with teachers and classmates through the internet

Business & Office Networks

Businesses use computer networks to connect employees and devices within an organisation. Networks allow workers to share files, printers, applications and internet connections, making communication and teamwork more efficient.

Hospitals & Healthcare

Computer networks help hospitals connect computers, medical devices and information systems. Healthcare professionals can securely share and access important information, supporting communication and the delivery of healthcare services.

Banks & ATMs

Banks use computer networks to connect branches, ATMs and banking systems. These networks allow customers to perform transactions, check account information and access banking services securely from different locations.

Social Media & Communication

Computer networks connect people around the world through social media, messaging applications and video calls. They allow users to send messages, share photos and videos, and communicate with others in real time.

Online Shopping

Online shopping platforms rely on computer networks to connect customers, websites, payment systems, warehouses and delivery services. Networks allow customers to browse products, place orders and make payments online.
