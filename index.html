Explore the World of Computer Networks

Imagine being able to connect computers, devices and people so they can communicate and share information efficiently. Computer networks make this possible by linking devices through wired or wireless connections. From accessing the internet and sharing files to supporting communication and online services, computer networks have become an essential part of the way we learn, work and connect in today's digital world.

What is a Computer Network?

A computer network is a system that connects computers, devices, and other digital equipment so they can communicate and share information. Networks can use wired or wireless connections to transfer data, access resources, and connect users to the internet.

Computer networks are an essential part of modern organisations, supporting communication, file sharing, internet access, and the use of digital services. By connecting devices efficiently, networks help people and businesses collaborate, exchange information, and stay connected in today’s digital world.

Why Do Computer Networks Matter?

Easy Communication

Connect computers and devices to communicate, exchange information, and access online services efficiently.

Resource Sharing

Share files, printers, applications, and internet connections easily between connected devices and users.

Essential Skills

Learning computer networking helps students develop valuable technical skills and prepares them for careers in the digital world.
